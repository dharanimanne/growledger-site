The monthly compliance dates every business should know

GrowLedger Advisors · November 2026 · 2 min read

Most penalties small businesses pay are for dates they simply forgot. These are the recurring deadlines that apply to most companies registered for GST and TDS.

Due dateWhat is due
7th of each monthTDS and TCS deposit for the previous month (30 April for March)
11thGSTR-1 for monthly filers
15thPF and ESI contributions
20thGSTR-3B and GST payment for monthly filers
22nd or 24thGSTR-3B for quarterly (QRMP) filers, depending on state
15 June, Sept, Dec, MarchAdvance tax instalments

On top of these come quarterly TDS returns, annual ROC filings, the GST annual return and your income-tax return. Late GST returns attract a late fee and 18% a year interest on tax paid late.

We keep a shared compliance calendar for every client, so nothing depends on memory.

This insight is general information based on the rules as we understand them in November 2026. Rules change, so check the latest notifications or speak to us before acting.
