Finance

Free cash from working capital before you borrow more

GrowLedger Advisors · November 2026 · 4 min read

Working capital is the cash your business needs to run day to day: money owed by customers and stock on hand, less what you owe suppliers. When it grows faster than sales, most businesses fill the gap with an overdraft or cash credit limit, and pay interest on it.

How much a few days are worth

For a business with ₹25 crore of annual sales, each day of sales is worth about ₹6.8 lakh. Collecting from customers 10 days faster frees about ₹68 lakh. At around 11% interest on a cash credit limit, that is roughly ₹7.5 lakh a year saved, every year.

Where to look first

  • Customer dues. Invoice the day the work is done, agree clear credit terms and follow up on a fixed weekly rhythm. Track overdue amounts customer by customer, not just the total.
  • Stock. Find slow-moving items and reorder levels that no longer match demand.
  • Supplier terms. Agree terms that suit your cash cycle, but pay micro and small enterprises within the legal limit of 45 days, or the expense is not deductible for tax until you pay.
  • GST credit. Credit missing from your GSTR-2B is cash you cannot use. Reconcile it every month.

Measure it every month

Three numbers tell the story: debtor days, inventory days and creditor days. Together they make your cash conversion cycle. If it is getting longer, your business is funding its customers.

A monthly MIS that shows these next to cash and borrowings lets you see a squeeze coming before the bank does.
