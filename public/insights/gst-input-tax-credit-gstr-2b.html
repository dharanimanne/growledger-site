Tax

Your GST credit only counts if it is in GSTR-2B

GrowLedger Advisors · November 2026 · 4 min read

You can claim GST input tax credit only on invoices that appear in your GSTR-2B, the monthly statement built from what your suppliers file. If a supplier files late, files wrongly or doesn't file at all, that credit is missing from your 2B, and claiming it anyway can lead to notices, interest and penalties.

Where credit usually leaks

  • Suppliers who file their GSTR-1 after the due date.
  • Wrong GSTIN, invoice number or amount on the supplier's side.
  • Invoices booked in your books but not accepted on the GST portal's Invoice Management System.
  • Credit on blocked items, such as certain vehicles and personal expenses, claimed by mistake.

A simple monthly routine

  1. Download GSTR-2B as soon as it is generated each month.
  2. Match it invoice by invoice with your purchase register.
  3. Send every mismatch to the supplier with a clear list and a date to fix it.
  4. Hold back payment of the GST portion to suppliers who keep failing to file, where your contract allows.

On ₹2 crore of input credit a year, recovering just 1% that would otherwise be lost is worth ₹2 lakh, and the routine takes a few hours a month.

This insight is general information based on the rules as we understand them in November 2026. Rules change, so check the latest notifications or speak to us before acting.
