Going global

Setting up in India: subsidiary, LLP or branch?

GrowLedger Advisors · November 2026 · 5 min read

Global companies entering India usually choose between four routes. The right one depends on what you plan to do here, how long you plan to stay and whether you will earn revenue in India.

RouteBest forKey points
Private limited subsidiaryMost businesses, including capability centresSeparate Indian company; foreign ownership allowed up to 100% in most sectors without prior approval; needs at least two directors, one resident in India
LLPSmaller service operationsForeign investment allowed only in sectors open to 100% investment without conditions; simpler compliance
Branch officeSpecific activities of the parentNeeds approval through an authorised bank under RBI rules; limited to permitted activities
Liaison officeMarket research and representationCannot earn income in India; expenses funded by the parent

What else to plan for

  • Transfer pricing between the Indian entity and the group, usually on a cost-plus basis for service centres.
  • FEMA reporting when the parent invests money or receives shares.
  • GST registration, and refunds where services are exported.
  • Payroll, provident fund and employee benefits from the first hire.

Getting the entity and the transfer pricing right at the start avoids costly restructuring later.

This insight is general information based on the rules as we understand them in November 2026. Rules change, so check the latest notifications or speak to us before acting.
