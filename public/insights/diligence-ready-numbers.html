Finance

Make your numbers diligence-ready before you raise

GrowLedger Advisors · November 2026 · 4 min read

In due diligence, investors and their advisors test every number in your pitch against your accounts, bank statements and filings. Gaps don't always kill a deal, but they slow it down and weaken your position on price.

Seven things to fix first

  1. Reconcile MIS to audited accounts. Revenue, margins and cash in your MIS should tie to the books, with differences explained.
  2. Revenue recognition. Be clear on when you book revenue, especially for subscriptions, advances and multi-year contracts.
  3. Customer data. Have clean data on customers, churn, concentration and receivables ageing.
  4. Statutory dues. Clear or disclose any unpaid GST, TDS, PF or ESI and old notices.
  5. Cap table and ESOPs. Every allotment, transfer and option should match the company's filings.
  6. Key contracts. Keep signed copies of major customer, vendor, lease and employment contracts.
  7. Related-party transactions. List them, with approvals in place.

Starting this three to six months before you raise usually makes diligence faster and gives investors fewer reasons to renegotiate.
