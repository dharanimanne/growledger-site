Where the business started

What we did

Step by step

Weeks 1–3

Diligence

Reviewed financial statements, tax filings, GST, payroll and statutory dues, and key contracts.

Week 4

Report

Quantified unrecorded GST exposure and unfunded employee dues, and their effect on price.

Weeks 5–6

Negotiate

Supported a price adjustment and specific indemnities in the agreement, working with the buyer’s lawyers.
