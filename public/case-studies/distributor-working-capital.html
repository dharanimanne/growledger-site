FMCG distributor · ₹25 crore in sales

Freeing ₹68 lakh of cash before raising the bank limit

Cash was always tight despite growing sales. Faster collection released cash and cut interest, without a bigger loan.

The situation

Where the business started

The business was about to ask its bank for a higher cash credit limit. Customers were taking around 75 days to pay against agreed terms of 45.

Nobody owned collections, and overdue amounts were tracked only as one total.

At a glance

FMCG distributor, ₹25 crore in sales

Discipline: Structure

Services: Virtual CFO services

What we did

Step by step

Weeks 1–3

Measure

Built debtor, stock and creditor days by customer and product, and a 13-week cash forecast.

Weeks 4–8

Fix the process

Invoicing on the day of dispatch, clear credit terms, a weekly collection rhythm with named owners, and escalation for overdue accounts.

Months 3–6

Hold the gain

Monthly working capital review in the MIS, with debtor days tracked against target.
