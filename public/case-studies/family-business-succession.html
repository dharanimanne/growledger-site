The situation

Where the business started

The founder held shares in three companies directly, alongside family members, with no will or shareholders’ agreement. Two children worked in the business; one did not.

Decisions were made informally, and nobody had discussed what would happen if the founder stepped back.

At a glance

Second-generation family group, three companies

Discipline: Strategy

Services: Family business advisory

What we did

Step by step

Month 1

Listen

Facilitated family meetings to agree goals, separating who would own, who would run and how decisions would be made.

Months 2–3

Design

Drafted a family constitution, evaluated a holding company against direct holding, and mapped the tax, stamp duty and company law steps.

Months 4–6

Implement

Coordinated wills and shareholders’ agreements with the family’s lawyers, and set up a board with an independent voice.
