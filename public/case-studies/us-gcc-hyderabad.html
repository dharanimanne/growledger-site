What we did

Step by step

Weeks 1–2

Choose the route

Compared subsidiary, LLP and branch, and recommended a wholly owned subsidiary with cost-plus remuneration.

Weeks 3–6

Set up

Incorporation, tax and GST registrations, bank account, payroll, provident fund and employment documents.

Operate

Transfer pricing policy with a safe harbour review, monthly US GAAP reporting pack, and a compliance calendar.
