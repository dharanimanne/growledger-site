The situation

Where the business started

The founders had a term sheet at a ₹40 crore pre-money valuation. Their books were three months behind, and the revenue model did not match the MIS.

Investors were asking for metrics the company could not produce quickly.

What we did

Step by step

Weeks 1–4

Get ready

Closed the books, reconciled MIS to accounts, and built a driver-based model with cohort and unit economics.

Weeks 5–8

Data room

Organised a complete data room and answered diligence questions within days, not weeks.

Weeks 9–12

Negotiate

Supported valuation discussions and proposed CCPS with milestone-linked conversion to bridge the gap. Handled FEMA and company law filings after closing.
