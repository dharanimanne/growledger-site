Applying the treaty rate on royalty paid abroad

Royalty to the overseas parent was taxed at the domestic rate. Treaty documents and transfer pricing support changed that.

The situation

Where the business started

The company paid ₹5 crore a year in royalty to its overseas parent and withheld tax at 20%. Nobody had checked whether the tax treaty allowed a lower rate.

At a glance

What we did

Step by step

Weeks 1–2

Review

Checked the treaty, the parent’s eligibility and the beneficial ownership position.

Weeks 3–6

Document

Obtained the tax residency certificate and Form 10F, and prepared transfer pricing support for the royalty rate.

Ongoing

Comply

Withholding at the treaty rate with the required filings, and annual transfer pricing documentation.
