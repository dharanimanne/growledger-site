Finding which customers made money, then pricing for it

Sales were growing but margins were not. Profit by customer showed where to reprice and where to cut cost.

The situation

Where the business started

Sales had grown for three years, yet profit was flat. The owner priced every customer from the same rate card and could not say which customers or products were worth keeping.

Freight, discounts and long credit for two large customers were not visible in the monthly accounts.

At a glance

Auto-components manufacturer, ₹25 crore in sales

Discipline: Strategy

Services: Virtual CFO services

What we did

Step by step

Weeks 1–4

Diagnose

Split revenue and direct costs by customer and product, then allocated freight, discounts, credit cost and service effort.

Weeks 5–8

Decide

Ranked customers by contribution after all costs. Identified a small group that was loss-making at current terms, and products whose prices had not kept up with input costs.

Weeks 9–12

Act

Supported the owner in repricing and renegotiating terms, and reviewed vendor and overhead costs. Set up a monthly profitability view in the MIS.
