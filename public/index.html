Where we add value

One firm for your finance, tax, legal and compliance, so nothing falls between advisors.

Growing businesses usually juggle a CA for tax, an accountant for books, a company secretary for filings and a lawyer for contracts. Nobody sees the whole picture, and the owner ends up holding it together.

GrowLedger brings them under one roof: Chartered Accountants, Company Secretaries, lawyers, registered valuers and cost accountants, working as one team. We take full ownership of that back office. One partner leads your work, brings in the right specialist when you need one, and keeps you ready for the next big step: a bank loan, a funding round, an acquisition or a move into a new market.

FAQ

Common questions.

If yours isn't here, ask us in a free 30-minute consultation.

What does GrowLedger do?

We are a partner-led advisory firm that looks after a business's finance, tax, legal and compliance needs in one place: accounting and MIS, tax and GST, company secretarial work, ESOPs, fundraising and due diligence, fund support, and India entry for global companies.

I'm a startup and need help with everything. Can you help?

Yes. That is exactly what we are built for. We can handle incorporation, accounting, payroll, tax, secretarial compliance and contracts, and support you through fundraising, all through one point of contact.

We already have a CA. Do we need to switch?

No. Many clients keep their auditor. We take on the parts you need and work alongside your existing advisors.

Can you help us set up a business in India?

Yes. We help global companies choose the right entity, incorporate, register for tax, set up accounting and payroll, and stay compliant afterwards, including transfer pricing with the parent company.

Do you help with fundraising?

Yes. We prepare your financial model, MIS and data room, support investor due diligence, and coordinate the transaction documents with our legal partners.

Do you work with investors and AIFs?

Yes. We support fund setup, due diligence on investments, fund operations and compliance, and exits.

Our cash is always tight even though sales are growing. Can you help?

Yes. We look at how long customers take to pay, how much stock you hold and your supplier terms, then set up a simple monthly view of cash and working capital. Releasing cash this way is often cheaper than increasing your bank limit.

Can you help plan succession in our family business?

Yes. We help the family separate ownership, management and control, and put the plan on paper: family constitution, shareholding and holding structure, and the tax and legal steps to transfer shares, working with your lawyers where needed.

Can you help reduce tax on payments to or from other countries?

Yes. We review cross-border payments such as royalties, fees and dividends, apply tax treaty rates where they are available, and set up group structures and transfer pricing correctly.

Is GrowLedger only a team of Chartered Accountants?

No. Our team brings together Chartered Accountants, Company Secretaries, lawyers, registered valuers, cost accountants and sector specialists. One partner leads your work and brings in whoever the task needs, so you deal with one firm instead of many.

How is your pricing structured?

Ongoing work is a fixed monthly fee based on your size and needs. One-time projects are quoted per project. Everything is set out in a written proposal before work begins.

Are there hidden fees or extra costs?

No. Government fees and any third-party costs are listed in the proposal, and anything new is approved by you before it is incurred.

Who will manage my account?

A named GrowLedger partner. They lead the work, review it and remain your main contact throughout.

Do I need to sign documents in person?

Usually not. Most documents can be signed electronically. Where a physical signature or notarisation is required by law, we arrange it with you.

How do you work with clients in cities where you have no office?

Most work is done online through video calls, secure document sharing and messaging. We travel for meetings where it makes a real difference.

How do you keep our data confidential?

Every engagement is covered by confidentiality terms, access to your files is limited to the people working on your account, and documents are shared through secure channels.
