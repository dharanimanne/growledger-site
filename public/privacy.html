Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

GrowLedger Advisors LLP (“GrowLedger”, “we”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through www.growledger.com, why we collect it and the choices you have, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

When you use our contact form, we collect the details you choose to give us: your name, company, phone or WhatsApp number, email address, the type of business you represent, the help you need and your message. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this website.

Why we use it

We use these details only to reply to your enquiry, to discuss and provide the services you ask about, and to keep a record of our communication with you. We do not sell your data or share it for marketing.

How it reaches us

Messages sent through the form are delivered to our email inbox by a form-delivery service (FormSubmit). If you choose to contact us by WhatsApp or email instead, those services handle your message under their own terms.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and, if you become a client, for the period our engagement and the law require. You can ask us to delete your enquiry at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct or update it, or have it erased, and you can withdraw consent for us to contact you. To do any of this, or to raise a concern, write to info@growledger.com and we will respond promptly.

Security

Access to enquiries is limited to GrowLedger partners and the team members who need it to help you.
