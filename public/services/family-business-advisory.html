Common questions

When should a family business plan succession?

Before it is urgent. Planning while everyone can still agree lets the family decide calmly; plans made after a health scare or dispute are made under pressure.

Does every family business need a holding company?

No. A holding company can simplify ownership and succession, but only when it fits what the family wants to achieve. The tax and legal costs of moving assets need to be weighed first.

Do you work with our existing lawyers?

Yes. We work alongside your lawyers and auditors, and bring in our legal partners where needed.
