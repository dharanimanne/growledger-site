Common questions

Why does GSTR-2B matter for my GST credit?

You can claim input tax credit only for invoices that appear in your GSTR-2B. If a supplier files late or wrongly, your credit is blocked until it is fixed, so monthly reconciliation protects your cash.

Can late payment to small suppliers increase my tax?

Yes. Payments to micro and small enterprises made after the legal deadline are deductible only in the year you actually pay, which can raise your tax for the year you booked the expense.

Do you handle tax notices?

Yes. We review the notice, prepare the reply with supporting documents and represent you where needed.
