Common questions

What does a virtual CFO do?

A virtual CFO does the work of a chief financial officer on a part-time basis: overseeing accounts and reporting, planning budgets and cash, advising on pricing, costs and funding, and dealing with banks and investors.

How is a virtual CFO different from an accountant?

An accountant records transactions and files returns. A virtual CFO uses those numbers to help you make decisions: where you make money, how much cash you will need and how to fund growth.

How much does a virtual CFO cost?

We charge a fixed monthly fee based on the size of your business and the scope of work, set out in a written proposal before we start.
