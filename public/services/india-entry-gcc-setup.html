Common questions

Which entity is best for setting up in India?

Most companies choose a private limited subsidiary, which allows up to 100% foreign ownership in most sectors. An LLP, branch or liaison office can suit narrower needs, each with its own approvals and limits.

How long does it take to set up a company in India?

Incorporation itself usually takes a few weeks once documents are ready. Bank accounts, tax registrations and payroll set-up follow, so plan for around one to two months before operations begin.

Can you work to headquarters’ time zones?

Yes. We support US, UK and European working hours and report in the formats headquarters needs.
