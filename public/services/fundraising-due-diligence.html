Common questions

When should we start preparing for a fundraise?

Ideally three to six months before you speak to investors. That gives time to close gaps in the books, build the model and organise the data room.

What is CCPS and why use it?

Compulsorily convertible preference shares convert into equity later, often at a ratio linked to milestones or a future valuation. They can help bridge a gap between what founders and investors think the company is worth.

Do you help with due diligence for investors too?

Yes. We support investors and funds with financial and tax due diligence on companies they plan to invest in.
