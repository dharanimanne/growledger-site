Common questions

How can a tax treaty reduce tax on payments abroad?

India’s tax treaties often set a lower withholding rate on royalties, fees for technical services, interest and dividends than domestic law. The lower rate applies only where the recipient qualifies and the required documents, such as a tax residency certificate, are in place.

What is transfer pricing?

When related companies in different countries trade with each other, the price must be what independent parties would agree. Transfer pricing rules require you to set and document that price every year.
